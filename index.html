Every piece begins as a conversation and ends in a box with your name on it. We guide each detail with care: privately, patiently, and entirely around you.

  1. i.

    Private consultation

    A one-to-one conversation, in person in Dubai or virtually, about your story, your occasion and your vision.

  2. ii.

    Design direction

    Sketches and references are shaped into a single design that is unmistakably yours.

  3. iii.

    Stone & material selection

    Diamonds, gemstones and metals chosen together, with certification when applicable.

  4. iv.

    Approval & creation

    Clear specifications and a final quotation are confirmed before the atelier begins; production starts after approval and deposit.

  5. v.

    Final presentation

    A last quality control, then your finished jewel is presented, and delivered worldwide, insured, with care.
